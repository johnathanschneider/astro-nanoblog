# Foreword

This page is pretty simple, it will be my outlet for my research and projects I work on. My digital notetaking for my thoughts on my work, my stream of consciousness, but public.  

Frankly, I would normally keep such a thing private, for the sheer sake that I find most blogs too self-interested. Why should the world be indulged to read my thoughts? What are special about my thoughts? I consider myself highly qualified, but to shove that into a public forum and insist I am feels nakedly arrogant.

Nonetheless, observationally I feel quite certain that it does improve one's public figure in an innocently appreciable way. Something about having a brand as a reputable engineer, and all.

If you are still interested in reading, please be forewarned that all text succeeding this shall be quite wordy. It is my unadulterated stream of consciousness on academic material, projects I want to work on, and research or studies of mine mostly printed for the publicity of the act itself. SEO loves this sort of thing, but I would think humans less so. I am not ashamed to state that I find my own writing here terribly obnoxious and if you are interested in my work or my thoughts, please jump straight to my projects, or email me directly if you'd like to discuss an actual topic without the kind of SEO-oriented Dickensian word-dump that shall follow.

Cheers
